A rule Hiren set: no cloud

The AI runs on his own laptop

Most AI lives on giant computers far away, and your words travel there. These notes are about a real money website, so they must never leave the machine. The whole brain runs offline, at home.

THE UPSIDE

Private and free. No bill, no secrets leaving the laptop.

THE CATCH

A home brain is smaller than a far-away one. It must fit in about 12 GB, so it is less clever. That is the trade.

The laptop is an M1 Pro with 32 GB of memory, but Hiren budgets it like a smaller 20 GB machine so it still works on his tiny second laptop too.

This is a real trade-off Nami can judge: private-and-small versus clever-but-sends-your-data-away. For secret work notes, private wins even though the AI is weaker. Ask Nami: when would you pick the private weaker helper, and when would you pick the smarter one that sees everything you type?
