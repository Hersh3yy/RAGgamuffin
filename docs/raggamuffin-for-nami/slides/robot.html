The three gates (stop 3's rules)

What the rule-checker throws out

The AI is eager and over-suggests. Three simple gates keep the notebook clean, so a worse AI still can't pollute it.

1

No code-names as ideas

If the "idea" is really just a file or class name, drop it. A concept is a thing, not a filename.

2

Proof must be real

Any code it points to must appear word-for-word in the note, or it gets cut.

3

Only the best 3

At most 3 concepts per chunk, keeping the ones with real proof first.

These gates live in code, not in the AI's instructions, so they are guaranteed, not just hoped for. Ask Nami: why put the rules in a reliable rule-checker instead of just politely asking the AI to behave? (Because the AI forgets; the rule never does.)
