Why it exists

Hiren has a drawer full of notes

At his job he writes long notes about how the money website works: how people sign, how payments go out. Right now there are about 30 of these, and they keep growing.

THE PROBLEM

The same idea, like "a signing", is explained a little bit in six different notes. Nowhere is there one clear page that says what a signing actually is.

THE DREAM

One tidy notebook with a single page per idea, every page linked to the others, so a person (or a helper AI) can find the real answer fast.

This is the "why". The notes are good but scattered, so finding one fact means reading six documents. Ask Nami: if you had to explain "what is a dog" but the answer was spread across six library books, what would you wish existed instead? (One page titled Dog.)
