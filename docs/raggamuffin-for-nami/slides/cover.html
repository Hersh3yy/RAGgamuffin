A tidy-up machine Hiren is building

RAGgamuffin

It takes a messy pile of work notes and turns them into a tidy, linked notebook that an AI can look things up in.

A patchwork quilt, sewn from scraps

RAGgamuffin is a side project. A raggamuffin is a kid in patched-together, ragged clothes, and that is the joke: the tool is built from scraps of notes stitched into something neat. Ask Nami: what is the messiest drawer or folder in your life, and what would "tidy" even look like?
