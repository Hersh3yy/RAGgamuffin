A true story: the first try flopped

The photocopier mistake

Version one took each note and passed it down a line of four AI helpers. Every helper rewrote the whole note in its own words and handed it on.

Each rewrite is a photocopy of a photocopy. The details blur first. One note that was 56 pages of real instructions came out as 9 pages, with the rest thrown away. 24 notes went in, 24 slightly-worse notes came out. No tidy single pages at all.

Hiren's own words at the time: the notes "pretty much become the same documents in the wiki." It was a document polisher wearing a knowledge-graph costume.

Honesty teaches more than a perfect story. The old design copied documents instead of building new pages. Ask Nami: if you retell a story to a friend, who retells it to another friend, five times over, what happens to the small details by the end? That is exactly what went wrong.
