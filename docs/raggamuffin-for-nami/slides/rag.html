The funny name: the RAG part

RAG means: the AI reads your notes before it answers

An AI on its own is like a kid doing a test from memory. It sounds sure even when it is wrong. RAG hands it an open book first: look it up, then answer.

CLOSED BOOK

Ask the AI straight. It guesses from memory and can make things up.

OPEN BOOK (RAG)

First it grabs the right notes, then answers using them. Grabbing is the "retrieval" in RAG.

RAGgamuffin does not answer questions itself. Its whole job is to build the tidy open book the AI will read from.

RAG is short for retrieval-augmented generation, but all Nami needs is "open book instead of closed book". Ask Nami: would you trust a friend who answers instantly from memory, or one who checks the book first? Why might the book-checker still get it wrong? (If the book is messy.)
